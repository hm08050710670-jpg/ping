Availability: TiSI drivers are no longer available.

At 323 cc., the TiSI driver was the largest, totally custom-fit titanium driver available. It was also one of the longest, as evidenced by the number of players who chose the TiSI at the National Long Drive Championships year after year.

Combining several unique, patented innovations in metal wood design, the TiSI helped golfers of all ability levels hit longer, straighter drives:

  • The Parallel Top & Sole creates a ridgid clubhead that transferred more energy to the ball than a conventionally-shaped driver, resulting in greater distance.
  • The Variable Thickness Face, thinner around the perimeter with more mass behind the ball, creates a high Coefficient of Restitution (COR).
  • The Multifunctional JX Hosel, available in 14 different configurations, combines face offset and lie angle to help a golfer gain directional control of his or her drives. A more upright and/or offset hosel position helps control fades and slices, while a flatter less offset hosel position helps control draws and hooks.
  • The Elliptical Backweight located directly behind the impact area creates a long "moment arm". This increases the interia, or resistance to twisting, and helps keep the face square and the loft stable when the ball is mishit.

TiSI Specifications

  • Head Size: 323 cc
  • Standard Length: 45"
  • Standard Swingweight Range: D4-D6
  • Lofts: 7.0°, 8.5°, 10.0°, 11.5°
