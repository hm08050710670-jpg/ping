The ZING 2 iron was introduced in January of 1994. Offering the key weighting features of the ZING iron with more rounded shape, the ZING 2 continued the emphasis on "extreme" weighting to optimize forgiveness for greater accuracy. The ZING 2's sole design not only complements the extreme weighting design, but also reduces the surface area in contact with the turf to promote clean ball contact.
