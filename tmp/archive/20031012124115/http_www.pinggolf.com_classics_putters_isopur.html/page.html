The Isopur series features a polyeurethane inset for softer feel without compromising feedback. By removing material from the face to create the cavity and distributing it to other areas of the putter, the putter's inertia was increased. The result is a putter that not only feels better, but is also more forgiving on mishits. Isopur 2 models have tungsten weights in the heel and toe to further increase the perimeter weighting.
