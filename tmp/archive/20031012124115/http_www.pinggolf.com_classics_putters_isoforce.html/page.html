The IsoForce series features a face insert consisting of 50 hexagonal-shaped pixels that creates exacting feel and feedback for the golfer. IsoForce models have a cast manganese bronze body with an Antique Bronze finish and an insert made from high-grade aluminum pixels. IsoForce 2 models have a stainless steel body with a copper pixel insert.
