Availability: Ti3 fairway woods are no longer available.

To complement the TiSI driver, we developed the Ti3 fairway woods. These woods utilize the same proven performance features of the TiSI, like the parallel top and sole, strategically-placed elliptical backweight, variable thickness clubface and a multi-position hosel.

Also made of titanium, these fairway woods have a zirconium soleplate and a pure tungsten bottom weight which combine to lower the center of gravity for optimium trajectory.

Ti3 Fairway Wood Specifications

  • Head Sizes: #3:190cc, #5:165cc
  • Standard Lengths: #3:43", #5:42.5"
  • Standard Swingweight Range: C9-D1.
  • Lofts: #3:14°, #5:17°
