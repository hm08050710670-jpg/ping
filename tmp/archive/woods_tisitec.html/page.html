Availability: TiSI Tec drivers are no longer available.

The TiSI Tec driver improved on the custom-fit TiSI titanium driver by making it even longer. Using new Chemical Milling Technology (CMT), the walls of the driver were made thinner and stronger in a process that removes the 'alpha' layer that forms during the casting process. The weight that was removed was redistributed in the form of weight pads that were cast into the soleplate. By positioning these pads close to the face in the heel and toe of the driver, our engineers were able to optimize the spin rate of the golf ball for a significant increase in distance.

New hosel positions were also introduced with the TiSI Tec. The JX hosels are available in 8 combinations of face angle and lie angle to help a golfer better control hooks and slices.

TiSI Tec Specifications

  • Head Size: 323 cc
  • Head Weight: 184 grams
  • Standard Length: 45"
  • Standard Swingweight Range: D2-D3
  • Lofts: ET 5.5°, ET 7.0°, ET 8.5°, ET 10.0°, ET 11.5°
    (ET stands for Effective Trajectory, not measureable loft)
